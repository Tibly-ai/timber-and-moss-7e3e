Tactile Learning

Inhabit the creative process in our Portland studio.

Gather around our hand-planed douglas fir workbenches. Guided by veteran woodworkers and botanists, you will craft lasting physical connections between forest material and refined domestic life.

Woodworking

Heirloom Timber Carving

3.5 Hours

Understand the grain, density, and natural oil content of native Oregon walnut and cherry. Under individual instruction, you will learn selection, hand-carving techniques, and preservation methods, leaving with a custom structural object designed to age gracefully over decades.

Botanical Design

Preserved Moss & Fern Panels

2.5 Hours

Master the art of vertical botanical composition. Work with sustainably harvested cushion moss, sheet moss, lichen, and preserved ferns to construct a framed biological tapestry. Learn structural balance, texture layering, and zero-maintenance preservation.

Private Engagements

Gather your team for a private creative session.

We host custom private workshops for corporate team gatherings, family events, and creative collectives. Each private session is designed in tandem with our instructors and includes curated organic refreshments, cheese boards, and local Oregon wine or botanical tea pairings.

Minimum booking of six participants is required. Contact our studio coordinator to arrange custom dates, times, and specific botanical or woodworking focuses tailored to your party.
