Slow Craftsmanship

The Journey from Tree to Living Space

We honor the native landscapes of Oregon. Our workshop operates strictly on sustainable material sourcing and meticulous organic preservation techniques.

01

Salvaging and Milling

We rescue native walnut, maple, and cherry slabs fallen in local storms or removed during orchard clearing. Each log is custom-milled at our studio, exposing wild live-edges and complex internal grain characteristics.

02

Stabilization and Air-Drying

Timber must dry naturally. We air-stack our green timber for up to twelve months before finishing it in our dehumidification kiln, ensuring structural stability so our work never cracks or warps.

03

Hand-planing and Finishing

Using traditional hand planes and natural oils, we highlight raw textures. No polyurethane layers or heavy plastic coatings are applied. We finish wood using organic bee wax and linseed oils for a satin feel.

04

Botanical Integration

Forest mosses are hand-harvested from private forest floor plots under local land permits. We preserve the moss using a non-toxic natural glycerin bath, locking in the vivid forest green color and pillowy soft texture indefinitely.
