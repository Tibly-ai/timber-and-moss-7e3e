Who We Are

Bridging the forest and finished stone.

Timber & Moss began with a simple observation: modern interior spaces often isolate us from the natural textures our senses crave. We wanted to construct a path back to the woods without sacrificing architectural refinement.

Established in the heart of the Pacific Northwest, our studio salvages fallen hardwoods and cultivates resilient forest mosses. Every piece we release preserves the wild, untamed gesture of the organic world while honoring clean geometric compositions that ground a room.

"Nature does not build in right angles, but when framed by them, its raw architecture becomes unmistakable."

Our Craft

The lifecycle of an honest object: from fallen giants to stable, living installations.

1

Responsible Sourcing

We partner with local arborists and certified foresters across Oregon to recover native trees that fell naturally or were cleared for safety. We never clear-cut, harvest living old-growth, or disrupt fragile native moss ecosystems.

2

Botanical Preservation

Our mosses undergo a specialized natural preservation process replacing water content with organic glycerins. They retain their lush texture and soft, vibrant quality indefinitely without requiring sunlight or watering.

3

Structural Joinery

We treat our wood with traditional joinery techniques, reinforcing splits with hand-carved bowties and sealing surfaces with plant-based natural hard-wax oils that protect the wood grain while remaining completely non-toxic.
